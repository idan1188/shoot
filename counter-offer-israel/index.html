Three numbers before you counter

Ask, target, and walk-away are a preparation heuristic. You choose them. They are not a market survey, not a legal floor, and not what anyone owes you.

01

Ask

The figure you open with in the reply. It is the first number in your counter, not a demand and not an average you found somewhere else.

02

Target

The figure where you would accept and feel the conversation did its job. Still a preference you set before you speak. Not a right.

03

Walk-away

The floor. Below it you would rather decline or keep looking. If this box is blank, you are not ready to send a counter.

Put the same three into the salary brief. It is the live tool, not a second opinion about what you deserve.

What to say when they push back

A salary negotiation counter offer script is a shape you rewrite in your own words. The notes below are examples and templates. They are not a guaranteed outcome, and they are not instructions an employer has to follow.

Example · template

Grateful, then specific

Use this shape when you want the role and you already know your ask and your target. Replace the brackets. Do not send the brackets.

Thank you for the offer. I want to join. The base I can move on is [ask]. If we can land near [target], I can confirm this week.

Example · template

They say the budget is fixed

This shape names the constraint and asks which other line can move. It does not argue that they must find more base.

I hear that the band is fixed. If base stays at [their number], which part can move — a sign-on, the timing of a review, or office days — so the package still clears my walk-away?

Example · template

You need a day, not a speech

How to counter a job offer includes not answering in the room if your walk-away is still blank. A short pause is a valid reply.

I want to reply carefully. I will send a short note tomorrow with the figure I am asking for and the reason.

Rehearse one of these against a lower reply in the command center. The tool practices the round. It does not predict what they will say.

Offer battle: base, bonus, equity, commute

In a salary negotiation in Israel the package is more than one line. Compare the lines. Do not add them into a single “total” this page never calculates. No line below is priced for you.

01

Base

The amount that repeats. A counter often starts here because bonus and equity can be delayed, reduced, or never paid. Base is the line your walk-away should be able to see on its own.

02

Bonus

Paid only if a condition is met. Ask what triggers it and when it is paid. A larger bonus does not automatically replace a lower base. This page does not forecast whether a bonus will be paid.

03

Equity

Shares or options are a claim on a later event, not cash this month. Keep them on their own line. This guide does not value a grant, a vesting schedule, or a future sale.

04

Commute

Office days, travel time, and whether you relocate are part of the package even when they are not a shekel line. A heavier commute can be a reason to hold your walk-away. A lighter one can be a reason you accept closer to their number.

Checklist, then the live tools

Use this as a preparation order. Stop if a step is blank. The last steps leave this page and open the calculators.

  1. Write the offer as they stated it: base, bonus, equity, commute, and the date they want an answer.
  2. Set ask, target, and walk-away before you reply. If the walk-away is blank, do not send a counter yet.
  3. Mark what you would trade — a review date, a sign-on, office days — and what stays fixed.
  4. Draft one short note from the example shapes above. It is your draft. It is not a script with a guaranteed yes.
  5. If they push back, answer the line that moved. Do not restart the whole package from zero.

Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employer, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this career, legal, or tax advice?

No. This is an educational preparation guide. It does not tell you what you are owed, what to accept, or how an employer must respond. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional.

What is a walk-away number?

The floor you set before you reply: below it, you would rather decline or keep looking. It is a preparation heuristic you choose. It is not a legal minimum and not a market statistic.

When should I not counter a job offer?

When you have already accepted, when the reply deadline is too close and you still have no walk-away, when the role is a no for reasons other than pay, or when countering would break a process you already confirmed. A decline, or a short pause, can be the cleaner move.

Will a salary negotiation counter offer script raise the offer?

No. The lines on this page are examples and templates for shape. They are not a promise of an outcome. An employer can hold the offer, revise it, or withdraw it.

What if they only move bonus or equity, not base?

Compare the lines separately. Bonus and equity are not the same as base salary. If base is below your walk-away and the extra is uncertain, the package has not cleared the floor you set. This page does not price shares, options, or a future bonus.
