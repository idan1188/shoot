Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the client, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this tax, legal, or career advice?

No. This page converts a figure you type. It does not tell you what to charge, which osek status to choose, or what a client must pay. Check a decision with the client and, where it matters, a qualified professional.

What is a typical hourly or monthly freelance rate in Israel?

This page does not publish one. There is no market survey, no average, and no suggested bill rate. The only shekel figures here are ones you type, and the conversion of those figures.

How do I convert hourly to monthly?

Choose hourly or monthly, type that figure, then type hours per week or days per month. If you choose days, also type the hours in a day you mean. A month from hours per week uses 4.33 weeks, the same factor as the rate calculator. The result is that arithmetic. It is not a rate this page recommends.

What if I type a utilization percent?

Utilization is optional. Leave it blank and the conversion uses every hour or day you typed. If you type a percent, the conversion uses only that share. This page does not supply a correct percent.

Does osek patur or osek murshe change this conversion?

Not on this page. The conversion does not add VAT, income tax, or national insurance, and it does not apply an osek uplift. Registration status changes what a client sees on an invoice. That path is the osek guide. The live rate calculator has a rough uplift you switch yourself.
