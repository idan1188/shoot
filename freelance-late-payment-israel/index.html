Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the client, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal advice or a debt-collection service?

No. This page helps you prepare a follow-up from details you type. It does not tell you that a client must pay, what interest applies, or which step to take next. It does not contact the client, file a claim, or collect a debt. Check a decision with the client and, where it matters, a qualified professional.

Will this page add a late fee or a legal threat?

No. There is no late fee, no interest rate, and no legal threat on this page. There is also no average collection time and no collection-rate statistic. The only amount in a draft is the invoice amount you type.

What do I type?

Client name and role are optional. Type the invoice amount yourself. Type a due date, a days-late count, or both. A note is optional. Blank optional fields stay blank or remain as brackets. This page does not fill them in, and it does not calculate days from the date.

Does ticking the checklist send the message or start a claim?

No. The ticks are prep notes. They do not send the email, the firmer follow-up, or the WhatsApp nudge, and they do not open a claim. Nothing you type or tick is sent.

What if brackets are still in the draft?

A bracket is a detail you have not typed, or a placeholder you have not replaced. The page flags it. Replace it yourself before you send anything. Copying a draft only puts the text on your clipboard. This page does not send it.
