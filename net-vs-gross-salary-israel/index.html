When the two labels are written, and when you are guessing

Orientation starts with a figure you can point to on paper. A percent you heard, or a take-home someone else quoted, is a guess. This page will not replace that guess with a bracket.

01

Written

You can point to a gross (ברוטו), an approximate net (נטו), or both, on a payslip, an offer, or a note you already made. The digits are yours. They are not a survey.

02

Questions

Short answers. They do not replace an accountant, a payslip, or an official source.

Is this tax advice or an official calculator?

No. This page is orientation for figures you already have. It is not tax, payroll, legal, or career advice, and it is not an official calculator. Check a real figure on the payslip, with an accountant (רואה חשבון), or with an official source such as the Israel Tax Authority (רשות המסים) or the National Insurance Institute (המוסד לביטוח לאומי).

Will this page turn gross into net, or net into gross?

No. If you type only a gross, that gross is repeated and the net stays blank. If you type only an approximate net you already have, that net is repeated and the gross stays blank. This page does not fill the empty box.

What is the average take-home salary in Israel?

This page does not publish one. There is no market salary, no average take-home, and no tax bracket here. The only figures on the page are the ones you type.

What does the difference mean if I type both numbers?

It is gross minus the approximate net, using only the two figures you typed. That subtraction is not income tax, not national insurance, not a health levy, and not pension. It does not approve either figure.

Do checklist ticks approve a figure?

No. A tick is a note that the line exists. Ticking every line does not approve a gross, a net, a difference, or a salary.

Where do I go for a raise, a first offer, or a promotion interview?

A raise talk is the raise guide. A first offer for a role you do not yet hold is the first job offer guide. A promotion interview in a role you already hold is the promotion interview guide.
