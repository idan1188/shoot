Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the client, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal advice?

No. This page helps you prepare a note from project details you type. It does not tell you that a client must pay a deposit, which percent applies, or which step to take next. It does not contact the client, and it does not collect money. Check a decision with the client and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only.

Will this page tell me what percent or amount to ask for?

No. There is no suggested percent, no suggested amount, and no Israeli average on this page. An amount or a percent appears only if you type it. A blank stays a bracket you replace yourself. This page does not turn a percent into a shekel figure.

What do I type?

Type a scope summary in your own words. Client, project label, a deposit amount, a deposit percent, and a note are optional. Blank optional fields stay blank or remain as brackets. This page does not fill them in.

Does ticking the checklist send the note or collect a deposit?

No. The ticks are prep notes. They do not send the deposit request, the start-after-paid note, or the short note, and they do not collect a deposit. Nothing you type or tick is sent.

Will these note shapes get the client to pay a deposit?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise of an outcome. A bracket is a detail you have not typed, including the amount and the percent. Replace it yourself. Copying a draft only puts the text on your clipboard. This page does not send it, and it does not include a payment phone number. A client can confirm, ask for a change, or decline.

Where do I go if this is the wrong page?

Converting an hourly or monthly figure you already use is the hourly to monthly page. Pricing the job from inputs you type is the rate calculator and Pricing OS.
