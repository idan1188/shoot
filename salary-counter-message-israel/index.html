When this page fits, and when it does not

Use this page when a numbered offer is already in front of you and you want an email or a short WhatsApp note filled from amounts and reasons you type. No number yet, unclear benefits, and the full counter strategy are different pages.

There is no number yet

Is this legal or HR advice, or a negotiation guarantee?

No. This page helps you draft an email or a short WhatsApp note from an offered amount, an ask, and reasons you type. It does not tell you whether to send the note, what to accept, or what the employer will do. It does not contact the employer. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only. They are not a promise that the offer will move.

Will this page tell me to ask a percent more?

No. This page does not publish an Israeli salary average, and it does not turn the two amounts into a percent, a gap, or a suggested raise. The offered amount and the ask appear only if you type them. A blank stays a bracket you replace.
