Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employer, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal or HR advice?

No. This page helps you draft an email or a short note from an offered amount, an ask, and reasons you type. It does not tell you whether to send the note, what to accept, or how the employer must respond. It does not contact the employer. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only.

Will this page tell me to ask a percent more, or quote an Israeli salary average?

No. This page does not publish an Israeli average, a market figure, or a rule to ask a percent more. An offered amount and an ask appear only if you type them, and a blank stays a bracket you replace. The page does not subtract the two figures, and it does not turn them into a percent.

What do I type?

Type what you already know. The offered amount, the ask, and up to three reasons are yours. Role, company label, and who receives the note are optional. A blank stays a bracket. This page does not fill a number, a reason, or a percent.

Does ticking the checklist send the message?

No. The ticks are prep notes about what you already know. They do not send the email or the short note. Nothing you type or tick is sent.

Will this message get the ask accepted?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise that the employer will raise the offer. A bracket is a detail you have not typed. Replace it yourself. Copying a draft only puts the text on your clipboard. This page does not send it, and it does not include a payment phone number. An employer can hold the offer, revise it, or withdraw it.
