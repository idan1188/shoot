Promotion interview · Israel · Preparation, not a promise

Prepare the promotion interview before you ask.

A promotion interview in Israel is a conversation about title or level in a role you already hold. It is not a first offer for a job you do not have, it is not a raise request that leaves the title alone, and it is not a retention counter. This page is when you are ready versus when you are guessing, notes you type yourself, and message shapes for email, WhatsApp, and verbal talking points. Educational preparation — not career, legal, or tax advice.

When you are ready, and when you are guessing

A promotion interview starts with notes you can point to. If the lines below are still a feeling, you are guessing. A round title does not make the conversation more prepared.

01

Ready

You already hold the role. You can write the current title, the target title or level, evidence in your own words, who is in the room, and what you will do if they say not now. The words are yours. They are not a survey.

02

Guessing

You have a percent you heard, someone else's title, or a sentence you have not written down. Opening with that is a guess dressed as a request. Pause and write the missing lines.

03

Wrong page

The offer is for a role you do not hold, you want more pay and the title is not the subject, or they already named a retention package. This checklist is for a promotion conversation in a job you have. Use the page that matches.

Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employer, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this career, legal, or tax advice?

No. This is an educational preparation guide. It does not tell you whether to ask, which title to request, what you should be paid, or how an employer must respond. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional.

What is a typical promotion, or the average raise that comes with one, in Israel?

This page does not publish one. There is no market survey, no suggested percent, and no typical title here. Current title, target title, and evidence are yours. Ask and floor, if you want numbers, are yours too. An average you heard somewhere else is not a floor.

When am I ready for the interview, versus guessing?

You are guessing while the checklist still has open lines: you already hold the role, the current title is written, the target title is written, evidence bullets are written, who is in the room, and a plan if they say not now. Ticking every line means the notes exist. It does not accept a promotion, a title, or a pay figure.

Will these email, WhatsApp, or talking-point drafts get me promoted?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise of an outcome. A manager can agree, defer, or say no.

How is a promotion interview different from a first offer, a raise, or a counter-offer?

This page is for a promotion conversation about title or level in a role you already hold. A first external offer belongs on the first job offer guide. A raise that leaves the title alone belongs on the raise guide. A retention package or a named package you are already replying to belongs on the counter salary offer guide. Use the page that matches the conversation.
