How does osek patur vs murshe change a freelance quote?

The freelance calculator uses a rough ×1.28 uplift for osek patur (no VAT line) and ×1.35 before VAT for osek murshe. Its illustration adds 17% VAT on the invoice and does not treat that as income. That is a pricing heuristic, not tax advice. Read osek patur vs murshe, then run your numbers in the live calculator.
