First job offer · Israel · Preparation, not a promise

Prepare the reply before you accept the offer.

A first job offer in Israel is an offer letter or a verbal package for a role you do not hold yet. It is not a retention counter from an employer you already work for, and it is not a raise request inside a job you have. This page is when you are ready versus when you are guessing, figures you type yourself, and message shapes for an email reply, WhatsApp, and a call opener. When you are ready, and when you are guessing

A first offer starts with notes you can point to. If the lines below are still a feeling, you are guessing. A round number does not make the reply more prepared.

01

Ready

You can point to the offer, write the offered base, an ask, a floor, who gets the reply, and what you will do if base does not move. The figures are yours. They are not a survey.

02

Guessing

You have a percent of the offer, someone else's number, or a sentence you have not written down. Opening with that is a guess dressed as a reply. Pause and write the missing lines.

03

Not yet

You have not been offered the role, you already said yes, or the package is a retention offer at an employer you already work for. This checklist is for before the reply, on a role you do not hold. Use the page that matches.

Which page matches the paper in front of you

A first offer, a counter-offer, and a raise request are different conversations. Use the page that matches. None of them is advice, and none of them promises an outcome.

01

This page — a role you do not hold

You have an offer letter or a verbal package for a job you have not started. You are preparing before you accept, ask a question, or negotiate. If you do not work there yet, stay here.

02

Counter-offer — retention, or a package already in a process

Use the counter salary offer guide when the named package is a retention offer from an employer you already work for, or when you are already inside a process and the job is to reply to that package. That page is the reply after the package is already in motion.

Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employer, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this career, legal, or tax advice?

No. This is an educational preparation guide. It does not tell you whether to accept, what you should be paid, or how an employer must respond. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional.

What is the average first salary in Israel?

This page does not publish one. There is no market survey, no percent of the offer, and no typical shekel figure here. Offered base, ask, and floor are yours. An average you heard somewhere else is not a floor.

When am I ready to reply, versus guessing?

You are guessing while the checklist still has open lines: the role is one you do not hold, the offered base is written down, an ask, a floor, who receives the reply, and a plan if base does not move. Ticking every line means the notes exist. It does not mean the offer is a good one, and it does not accept it.

Will these email, WhatsApp, or call templates raise the offer?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise of an outcome. An employer can hold the offer, revise it, or withdraw it.

How is a first job offer different from a counter-offer or a raise?

This page is for an offer letter or a verbal package for a role you do not yet hold. The counter salary offer guide is for a retention offer from an employer you already work for, or for a named package you are already replying to inside a process. The raise guide is for asking for more pay in a job you already have, before anyone has named a new package. If they asked for salary expectations and no written offer is in hand, use the salary expectations guide. Use the page that matches the paper in front of you.
