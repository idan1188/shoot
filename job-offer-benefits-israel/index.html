Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employer, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal, tax, or HR advice?

No. This page helps you line up benefit lines from wording you type. It does not tell you whether a pension line, a vacation line, a bonus, or an equity line is enough, and it does not tell you which step to take next. It does not contact the employer, and it does not accept the offer. Check a decision with the employer and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only.

Will this page tell me the pension percent or a standard package in Israel?

No. There is no pension percent, no vacation count, no Israeli average, and no standard package on this page. A benefit line appears only if you type what the letter already says. A blank stays a bracket you replace yourself.

What do I type?

Type the wording already on the offer letter. Role, who receives a note, base, pension, vacation, car or phone, bonus, equity or RSU, another line, and a note are optional. Blank optional fields stay blank or remain as brackets. This page does not fill them in.

Does ticking the checklist accept the offer or send the questions?

No. The ticks are prep notes. They do not send the clarifying questions, the blank-lines note, or the short note, and they do not accept the offer. Nothing you type or tick is sent.

Will these note shapes get the benefit lines changed?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise of an outcome. A bracket is a detail you have not typed. Replace it yourself. Copying a draft only puts the text on your clipboard. This page does not send it, and it does not include a payment phone number. An employer can confirm, correct a line, or decline to discuss it.

Where do I go if this is the wrong page?

Answering a named number, or a retention package, is the counter salary offer guide. Ask, floor, and the reply before you accept a role you do not hold are the first job offer guide. Employee versus contractor, before a status is chosen, is the contractor versus employee guide. Asking for more pay when nobody has named a new package is the raise guide. The salary brief and the command center are the live desks. A purchase link is an email: salary is ₪26 (was ₪79), freelance is ₪23 (was ₪69), and the bundle is ₪43 (was ₪129). There is no checkout on this site, and there is no payment phone.
