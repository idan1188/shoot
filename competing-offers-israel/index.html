When this page fits, and when it does not

Use this page when two written offers are already in front of you and you want them side by side from bases and extra lines you type. One numbered offer, unclear benefits, and silence after an interview are different pages.

Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the employers, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal, HR, or tax advice, or a decision?

No. This page lines up two written offers from a base and one extra line you type for each. It does not tell you which offer to take, what to accept, or what an employer will do. It does not contact either employer. Check a decision with the employers and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only. They are not a decision.

Will this page tell me which offer to take?

No. This page does not publish an Israeli salary average, and it does not rank the two offers. It does not subtract the two bases or turn them into a rate. Each base and each extra line appears only if you type it. A blank stays a bracket you replace.
