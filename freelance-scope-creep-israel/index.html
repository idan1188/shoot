Questions

Short answers. They do not replace a conversation with the client, or with a qualified professional when the decision needs one.

Is this legal advice?

No. This page helps you prepare a note from project details you type. It does not tell you that extra work must be paid, which clause applies, or which step to take next. It does not contact the client. Check a decision with the client and, where it matters, a qualified professional. The lines are drafts and preparation only.

Will this page tell me what to charge for the extra work?

No. There is no suggested fee, no percent of the original quote, and no Israeli average on this page. Hours you type stay a count. A fee already written on the quote is repeated only if you type it. A fee for the later items stays a bracket you replace yourself.

What do I type?

Type the original scope and what appeared later, in your own words. Client, project label, hours, a fee already written, and a note are optional. Blank optional fields stay blank or remain as brackets. This page does not fill them in, and it does not turn hours into a shekel figure.

Does ticking the checklist send the note or approve a change?

No. The ticks are prep notes. They do not send the clarifying note, the re-quote shape, or the short note, and they do not approve a change or a price. Nothing you type or tick is sent.

Will these note shapes get the client to accept the change?

No. The lines are templates to rewrite. They are not scripts that work, and they are not a promise of an outcome. A bracket is a detail you have not typed, including any new fee. Replace it yourself. Copying a draft only puts the text on your clipboard. This page does not send it. A client can confirm, ask for a change, or decline.

Where do I go if this is the wrong page?

An unpaid invoice you already sent is the late invoice guide. A first quote, before scope is agreed, is the freelance quote guide. Employee versus contractor, before a status is chosen, is the contractor versus employee guide. Converting an hourly or monthly figure you already use is the hourly to monthly page. Pricing the later items from inputs you type is the rate calculator and Pricing OS. The command center is the other live desk.
